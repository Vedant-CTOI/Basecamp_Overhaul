Team Select — four motion studies

Same stage, four ways it can move. Each mock is live — it loops on its own or answers a click. Red is the platform's voice; motion is an event, never a texture.

The drum advances like a film reel — one crisp 500ms swap, a single frame of flicker on the incoming coin, and a counter stamp under the stage.
